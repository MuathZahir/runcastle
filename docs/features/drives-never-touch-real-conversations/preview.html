Drives never touch real conversations

A test drive works on a copy of your runcastle and stays there: it never wakes, prompts or switches a real agent, never pings your phone, and a merge conflict gets resolved once.

The show

What happens when you start a drive of runcastle while a round burns in your live install.

Drive setupCopies your live database, conversation ids, running rounds, phone subscriptions and all.
Drive's server bootsCalls your live round "interrupted by a server restart" and wakes its lead by resuming the real transcript.
Your real leadActs on fake news: re-dispatches a round that is still burning, waits on you for a false reason, gets its model switched.
Your real orchestratorHears the drive's copy of your live drive as a "leftover" and stops it.
Your phoneGets pings for things that happened only in the copy.
Drive setupCopies your live database and marks it as a copy. 1
Drive's server bootsCuts the copy loose first: conversation ids cleared 2, running rounds closed quietly 3, your live drive not recovered 4, phone subscriptions dropped 5. Everything else stays 6.
In the driveChats show their real history. Your first message starts a fresh agent. Background news wakes nobody you have not started 9.
Your real agentsHear nothing. The live round finishes undisturbed.

How it gets built

Small enough to build myself in this chat, no worker tickets. Then one review round (a code review: the change has no screen to drive), then you drive this branch while a round burns in your live install.

What was decided

What is left out

Later

  • Drives never touch real repositories (offered as a backlog feature): a drive still moves real branches when you create, land or merge in it

Not this feature

  • The real server's own restart cleanup mistaking a live burn for a dead one (its own draft)
  • A separate Claude config dir for drives
  • Drives of branches cut before this merges, until they take main

Under the hood
Drive setup writes one setting, RUNCASTLE_DATABASE_IS_COPY=1, into the drive's environment when it copied a database.
Server boot, with the setting on, right after migrations and before reconciliation, in one transaction:
  • clear every session's conversation id (Claude Code session or Codex thread); keep its transcript path
  • close running runs and their burning tickets; no events, no wakes, no git
  • delete phone push subscriptions
  • skip recovering cut-off drives
Delivery, with the setting on: a background runcastle prompt to a thread with no conversation of its own starts nothing and records why. Clicks and typing by the driver still start agents.
Orchestrator news: the merge-conflict line says who merged; merge.resolved becomes news. Feature skill and resolve kickoff: check the base is not already in the branch first.

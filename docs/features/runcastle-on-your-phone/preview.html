How it gets built

One round of workers. The first three run at once, and live design follows them.

1 · The phone's list and chatThe width switch, list, chat bar, sheets, full-screen views, warning
2 · Chat, touch and read-onlyEnter and hints, Plan and Tickets without controls, package, hidden drive
3 · The private linkTailscale service, Settings section, QR code
4 · Live designAfter 1 and 3. Can split off if it fights back
5 · ReviewWalks the whole thing at phone width

Then you try it on your actual phone before merge.

What is left out

Later

  • A phone-friendly picture viewer: goes with "Runcastle on your phone"
  • Keeping the review's app running: stays in the older feature, whose lead drops its tickets 2 and 5

Not this feature

  • Any automatic comparison of screens
  • Any server rule requiring a review, or holding a merge for one
  • Pictures from a video, or live while a review runs
  • Cleaning up old review folders

Drive chats become workers

The Fix-drive chat and the scenario chat beside a test drive become workers: listed with the feature's workers, with a status, a header and a handback to the lead. They still run in your checkout, beside the drive, exactly where they work today.

Under the hood
WorkerKind+ fix-drive, scenario

Host placementdrive review, fix-drive, scenario run in the drive's checkout; the rest sandboxed
→
OwnerfeatureId nullable + projectId (migration)
  • The drive routes (feature.fixDrive, feature.openScenarioChat) keep their inputs and call one worker-service entry that finds or creates the owner's worker of that kind and sends the ask.
  • Host handback reuses the drive review's handback file; status goes to handed-back, never landed; land, send back and drop refuse.
  • The drive's stop ends drive workers' turns and writes their handback; retry_drive does not count as a stop.
  • Guards unchanged: edit guard (.runcastle/, .gitignore), checkout guard, bypassPermissions on Claude Code. Model from the "Drive chats" setting.
  • Drive workers skip the "agents working at once" slots and the account hold; they get the worker runner's retry, stall and restart nets.
  • The drive-fix and scenario session kinds are no longer created; old rows read as "Old drive chat" and take no message.

Who builds what

  • Me · the server's project-wide list of live workers, so every feature row has its workers. Small, done first.
  • Sidebar rows · with you nested worker rows, fold chevron, "+" on hover, the drill-in page removed. Taste work: you shape the look with it until it reads professional and native to the sidebar.
  • Review · on its own runs the checks and drives the app at the end; you get its package before the test drive.

Under the hood
  • Server · a new worker query takes a project and returns every feature's live workers (not handed back, landed or dropped), each with its feature id. It refetches on every worker event. The feature list's "workers page" count, which only fed the arrow, goes.
  • Sidebar · the feature row gains its worker rows (today's worker row, nested), a fold chevron remembered per feature in this browser, and a hover "+". This happens everywhere a feature row is drawn: lanes, shelves, under epics.
  • Open feature · its done workers and old chats come from what its page already publishes, folded as "N done".
  • Removed · the drill-in page, its header and Lead card, the "›" arrow, and the sidebar's down/up state.

Waiting is not backlog

"Not now" and "yes, as soon as X ships" stop sharing one place. A waiting feature sits in its own Up next group and starts by itself when its last dependency merges, so nothing you wanted gets forgotten in the backlog.

The show

Two ideas, two places

Today one backlog holds both. After this, each has its own meaning.

Backlog: "not now"

  • Stays until you click Start.
  • May have dependencies; they only say whether it could start.
  • Never asks for your attention.

Up next: "yes, after X" 1

  • Needs something unshipped to wait on. 2
  • Starts by itself when the last one merges. 3
  • If it can't start, it asks you. 5

What happens at the merge

No session launches and no tokens are spent. A failure never fails the merge.

You click Merge on XIts page listed "Starts when this ships: Y". 13
X shipsWas X the last thing Y waited on?
Y starts like your Start clickBranch cut from the branch X merged into. 4
It workedY moves to the live lanes and needs planning. Both timelines get a line. 6
It couldn'tY shows in Needs you with Start and the reason: start failed, mixed branches, dependency archived or deleted. 5

Chains go one step at a time: C's merge starts B, and A keeps waiting on B. 14 There is no unstart; archive is the way out. 15

Under the hood

Storage. One new boolean column on features, meaningful only while status = 'draft'; cleared when the feature starts. No new status, no change to feature_dependencies.

Rules (core, IO-free). "Up next" = draft + waiting mark + at least one unshipped dependency, none archived. "Stuck" = draft + waiting mark + (nothing unshipped, or an archived dependency). Both derived at read time; nothing about satisfaction is stored.

Auto-start. In mergeFeatureFlow, after the feature ships: for each waiting dependent with nothing unshipped left, check its dependencies' bases agree, then call the same start path as the Start click with that base. Wrapped so it never fails the merge; a failure records an event whose reason the Needs you row shows.

Wire. FeatureListItem and FeatureFull carry the mark; a feature.setWaiting tRPC procedure backs the two buttons; MCP create_feature and set_feature_dependencies gain waiting.

Web. sidebar.ts gains an upNext triage key and lane, new chips, and the stuck case in needsMe; the draft next-step resolver gains the two actions; the dependency page's Blocks line splits.

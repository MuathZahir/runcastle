Under the hood

The page at rest is composed in the project workspace from these pieces, top to bottom:

  • PageHeader: the mark, "Orchestrator", the facts line, and OrchestratorDoors (new) on the title's line: the drive's states, the landing menu, New chat, and a caption hung beneath.
  • NotesCard gains a section form for the page; the panel's form stays. NoteDot gains a narrow column for lists with no pictures.
  • ConversationList groups by the pure conversationGroup(thread, now); a filter past five; no Show more.
  • DriveSetup (new): the disclosure lifted out of the old Test drive card.
  • The panel: at rest it offers only a running drive and never opens by itself; around a chat it offers Notes as today. The note capture's View and a drive's Stop land accordingly.
  • Deleted: NewChatCard, TestDriveCard, ActionRow, their still-true tests moved to the new pieces. DESIGN.md's project-page paragraph rewritten.

Built by the lead in this checkout, no workers: the design already lives in the real components, so what is left is tests, deletions, the docs paragraph and a check of View and drive stop.

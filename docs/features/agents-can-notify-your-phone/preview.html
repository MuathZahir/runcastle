Agents can notify your phone

A tool lets an agent send you a phone notification when it judges you are needed. Nothing is sent automatically, and it works for anyone running runcastle.

How you'll try it on your phone

  1. Start the test drive and turn on the phone link in the drive's Settings.
  2. Scan the code on your Android phone and switch on Notifications. A test notification arrives.
  3. Ask the drive's Orchestrator to notify you, then tap what arrives.

What is left out

Later

  • Nothing is parked for later

Not this feature

  • History, an inbox, or per-project settings
  • Automatic pings on any event
  • Images in a notification
  • A limit on how many an agent sends
  • Notifications without the phone link
  • A real iPhone trial: built to the standard, untested here

Under the hood
notify_human({ message })New MCP tool for chat and project sessions. Refuses on a held feature and over 200 characters.
Push serviceVAPID keys and a subscriptions table in the database. Sends with web-push, drops expired phones.
tRPC routerPublic key, subscribe (sends the test), unsubscribe.
Service workerShows the push, handles the tap, and gives failed page loads the "can't reach your computer" page.

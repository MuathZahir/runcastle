Which orchestrator news wakes, and which waits 3 4 14

Wakes: work came back, or something only the orchestrator can answer. The list is fixed.

Wakes

  • a lead waits for the human
  • a lead declared ready to test
  • a round failed
  • a merge conflicted
  • a test drive failed to start
  • the human merged a feature
  • a backlog feature started by itself, or could not
  • runcastle could not wake a lead
  • a planning run ended and nothing waits yet

Waits for the next turn

  • the human is talking to a chat / it went quiet
  • the human started or stopped a drive
  • the human had a feature plan itself
  • a planning run ended after a wait went out
  • a round finished, or a restart cut it off
  • a review pass finished or failed
  • another thread took the feature

Same mechanism as lead-chats-stay-small, built here last, once that feature is on main. Holds and held lines stay put; no new chats appear. Decisions 7, 8 and 15 are replaced.

News that hits the usage limit is sent again at reset 9

runcastle's message starts a turn→ "You've hit your limit"→ message held again→ one wake at the reset time

Once per usage window at most. A human's own message is left alone.

What is left out

Under the hood
event→ orchestrator notifier: line + wake/hold→ 300 ms gather→ any wake: one prompt (held lines ride first) all hold: thread host hold
  • Thread host: the held preface becomes an ordered list of entries on disk, each with an optional replace key; a usage-limit check on turns runcastle started, with one timed wake at the runtime's reported reset.
  • Orchestrator notifier: a wake/hold class per line, the dead "headless run" lines removed, and the resume-cap check before a wake to a stopped holder.
  • Compaction: lead-chats-stay-small's automatic triggers extended to orchestrator threads, with orchestrator keep-instructions; the launcher's 1 MB resume cap no longer starts fresh orchestrator chats.
  • Lead delivery: Stop held for an idle lead; the round-return message's closing line.
  • Web: the news block's "For its next turn" label from a held flag on the prompt display.
  • Skills: the project skill's foreground-reads guidance; the feature skill's round guidance.
  • Built by the lead in its own checkout (one connected change, one burn at a time on this project), then one review ticket. Checked by hand-starting this branch's server, since live sessions run the installed runcastle.

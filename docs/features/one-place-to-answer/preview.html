Under the hood
  • create_feature: the epic owns the feature exactly when finish ≠ you; planning: with-me plus an epic finish is refused.
  • wait_for_you under an epic owner: releases to the human (new owner-change cause, finish → you, one event, handback cleared), wakes the epic with its one line, and drives the hand-over line in the feature chat.
  • hand_back loses planAgreed; the pass-at-plan-yes step goes.
  • send_to_feature loses relaysHuman and its header.
  • start / plan_feature allowed on a human-owned feature whose chat_id is the caller, brief "Planning mode: automatic", never planned.
  • One-time boot move: human-owned features with finish ≠ you get you; epic-owned untouched.
  • Web: the owner reading loses its planning state; menu hints gain the rule.

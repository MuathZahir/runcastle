Under the hood
  • State. The epic's chat gets an "autopilot on" switch; each feature gets "in its epic's run". Locked = both. The feature's epic is the chat that created it, a link that exists today. The per-feature owner and finish columns are dropped.
  • One check replaces the hold refusals: off autopilot an epic may create, start, plan and read its features; on, it may also message, stop, drive and merge the ones in its run.
  • Tools. "Take a feature" goes. A new orchestrator tool turns autopilot on with its list, adds or removes a feature, and turns it off. The "End autopilot" link calls the same service.
  • Leads. On autopilot their hand-back and their wait-for-you both go to the epic and set no need on the feature. A dead lead run is reported to the epic.
  • Typing. The hook that made a feature yours when you typed becomes a refusal on locked chats; the web shows no message box there.
  • Records. A new ADR supersedes ADR-0016 and ADR-0021.

Under the hood

Round 1 now: Tickets, then the frame and docs. Round 2 once “The chat looks the part” has merged and this branch has main: retiring Review, whose warnings go into the chat’s new notice strip, then a review. The designs are committed, so workers connect them without restyling.

#1 Tickets
TicketsView takes every action RunBody and TicketsBody had (stop, retry, model, edit, cancel, set aside, the live log, one model for all); the review ticket’s findings and walkthrough; RunBody, the run lanes and the ledger retire.
#3 Frame and docs
The open doc in the panel’s state and the reader in every view; DocPeek and the grill pane retire; Tabs gets a tooltip; DESIGN.md and STYLE.md.
#5 Review retired (round 2)
Review removed from the panel model, /review lands on Tickets; its notices in the chat’s strip; ReviewBody, ShippedBody and their bands retire; notes in Details unless the drive already has them; Merged in Details.
#6 Review (round 2)
Drives Plan, Tickets and Details against these criteria.

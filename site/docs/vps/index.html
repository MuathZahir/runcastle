Another machine or your phone

Put runcastle on a server so features keep building while your laptop sleeps, and check on them from your phone.

Get started How a feature goes The orchestrator Another machine or your phone

On a server

  1. Log in as a normal user with sudo; the install refuses root.
  2. Run the install. On a server, let it answer for you: 
    $ curl -fsSL https://runcastle.dev/install.sh | bash -s -- --yes --agent claude
    Use --agent codex or --agent both for the others.
  3. Log out and back in, so the docker group applies.
  4. Sign in: claude auth login then runcastle token, or codex login.
  5. Keep it running: runcastle service install. It starts at boot and restarts if it stops.

Reach it

It has no login and listens on 127.0.0.1 only, so never expose it. Forward both ports over SSH, then open http://localhost:4512: 

$ ssh -L 4512:localhost:4512 -L 4514:localhost:4514 user@host

Port 4514 serves designs. Without it the Design view stays empty.

Move an existing setup

runcastle move user@host carries your projects, every branch, uncommitted work and runcastle's own data to a Linux or macOS machine. Add --dry-run to look first.

On your phone

Install Tailscale on the machine and your phone. In runcastle, open Settings → General → On your phone, turn the link on and scan the QR code. It stays on your own Tailscale network, never the internet.

Add the page to your home screen and turn on notifications: a feature that needs you then reaches your phone. On an iPhone they work only from the home screen.

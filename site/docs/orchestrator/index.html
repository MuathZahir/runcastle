The orchestrator

The orchestrator is the project's own chat, above the features. Bring it anything that is not a feature yet: an idea, a bug report, a complaint from a user.

Get started How a feature goes The orchestrator Another machine or your phone

Bring it an idea

Paste a paragraph, a screenshot or a message from a user. The orchestrator checks it against what is already built or being built, asks about what is new, and turns that into one or more features.

The backlog

Features you are not ready to start wait in the backlog, as a title and a short description, with no branch yet. Press Start on one to create its branch and open its chat.

Notes

A note is anything you spot while using your app or runcastle: a bug, something confusing, an idea. Press Ctrl+J (⌘J on a Mac) anywhere in runcastle to write one, or use the notes panel during a test drive.

Triage then goes through the open notes with you and sends each one where it belongs: a new feature, a batch of small fixes, a feature already being built, or nowhere.

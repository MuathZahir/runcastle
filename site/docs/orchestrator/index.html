The orchestrator

Every feature has its own chat. The orchestrator is the one above them, for an idea before it is a feature.

Get started How a feature goes The orchestrator Another machine or your phone

Bring it an idea

Paste a complaint, a screenshot or a paragraph. It checks what is already built and in flight, asks about the rest, and cuts it into features.

The backlog

A feature you are not ready for waits in the backlog: a title and a brief, no branch yet. Start opens its planning chat.

Notes

Jot what you notice with Ctrl+J (⌘J on a Mac) anywhere in the app, or while you test drive. Triage goes through them with you and sends each one on: a new feature, a batch of small fixes, a feature already in flight, or nowhere.
